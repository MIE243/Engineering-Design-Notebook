## Summary
Developed CD3 as a manually operated teaching drivetrain model supported above a stationary base. The concept aims to expose differential and transfer-case motion while allowing students to control the input speed by hand.

## Sketch and concept development
![[2026-10-04 CD3 Concept Sketch.png]]

The upper sketches explore the wheel and drivetrain arrangement, support shaft, base, and a handle/selector near the transfer case. The annotated open-differential reference highlights different output speeds and motivates making the internal mechanism visible.

The lower sketch develops a separate hand input and transfer-case selector, together with front and rear axle differentials. A transparent enclosure is proposed so students can see the mechanism. Vacuum forming and resin 3D printing are noted as possible enclosure manufacturing methods, rather than selected processes.

The lower-right illustration presents a possible hand crank, lock selector, transparent transfer case, axle differentials, and pedestal base. It is a concept visualization, not a completed CAD design or verified mechanism.

## Intended demonstration
- Students turn the hand crank to drive the mechanism at a controllable speed.
- Students compare output-wheel motion while deliberately applying different resistance to the outputs, using a suitable safe resistance method to be developed.
- A selectable lock arrangement could support comparison of open and locked differential behaviour, subject to detailed design.

## Design considerations and next steps
- Define the hand-input transmission, gear ratios, selector functions, and locking mechanism.
- Determine a safe method of applying output resistance without placing fingers near exposed gears.
- Verify that the base remains stable during cranking and that the support does not obstruct the drivetrain.
- Compare transparent-enclosure manufacturing options for visibility, strength, fit, and cost.
- Plan visible wheel markers and an observation check to assess whether students can distinguish output speeds.
